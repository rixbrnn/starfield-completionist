# Completionist for Starfield

An in-game menu that tracks every quest, activity and errand in Starfield and shows what the player is still missing in each system, planet and city.

## The menu

**View**:
One of the menu's top-level screens (Log, Here, Locations, Quests, Side Quests, Activities), switched from the bar at the top.
_Avoid_: Menu, header tab, top tab

**Category tab**:
One of the boxed tabs above the quest list that narrows it (All, In progress, Not discovered, and the Log's Main, Faction, Side...).
_Avoid_: Sub-header, filter tab, sub-tab

**Quest list**:
The right-hand column of a view: the quests in the current place or questline, grouped by status, with the open quests' objectives under them.
_Avoid_: Mission list, the list

**Objective**:
One step of a quest as the game's mission log shows it, listed under its quest when the quest is open.
_Avoid_: Sub-objective, sub-quest, sub-mission, task

## Distribution

**Edition**:
One packaged version of Completionist, defined by the channel it ships through. Every edition tracks quests the same way; editions differ in how they show progress and what they need installed.
_Avoid_: Version (that's a release number), port, build

**Nexus edition**:
The edition on Nexus Mods, for PC players with SFSE, OSF UI and OSF Settings, shown in the HTML menu.
_Avoid_: PC version, full version

**Creations edition**:
The edition on Bethesda's Creations, for any player without SFSE: Xbox, PS5 and PC (Steam or Game Pass), shown inside the game's own Missions menu.
_Avoid_: Console version, completionist-console, lite version
